Island House

Heritage · Grade II*

Island House isn't just a beautiful building with a famous medieval quay underneath it. We've traced its continuous, documented 800-year human story — every owner and occupier from the 1300s to today — across 29 source-checked research threads — now a live, growing register of 2,034 sourced facts, 895 people & places and 748 sources — and graded every claim by evidence. Before this, it was "an old merchant's house" and a handful of legends. Now it's a defensible archive you can stand behind — and question directly, below.

How it all ties together

This isn't a handful of separate projects — it's one connected body of research about one place. Across 158 documents and ~227,000 words, we've traced the deep history of Laugharne and Island House together: the castle and the medieval borough, the quay, the notable townspeople and the town's war dead, the old industries, and the buildings along King Street. Island House is the landmark — and we hold even more on the town around it (~115,000 words) than on the house itself (~83,000).

The Globe sits right inside that story. We've researched the Globe's own building — its ballroom, its coach-house courtyard, the 1869 sale — and Brown's Hotel next door with its Dylan Thomas association. So the history doesn't just decorate the town; it gives every venue, the Globe included, a real story to tell.

That's exactly what the township trail app is built to do: turn the research into footfall. It hooks visitors with verified stories of the buildings around them, walks them through the town — and routes them to the Globe's door.
