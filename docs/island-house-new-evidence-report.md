# Island House — the new evidence we've found

*Island House desk · 24 Jul 2026 · a quick report. Every item traces to a source we've read; tiers are honest — verified vs still-confirming is marked.*

## The building itself — older and better-evidenced than the record said
- **The roof timbers date to 1437** (dendrochronology + radiocarbon). The old site calls it "16th century" — the science makes the fabric **15th-century, a century older.** ✅ Verified.
- **The earliest medieval quay yet excavated in Wales** sits on the plot (Heneb dig), and imported pottery shows the house **traded with Bristol, the French coast and Spain.** ✅ Verified.

## The people — a documented ~800-year line where the old record began at 1778
- The ownership runs through Laugharne's leading families: **White** (14th-century landholders, likely the original builders) → **Prydderch → Thomas → Hughes → Jane Abra Hughes**, the heiress who carried the house to the **Skyrmes**. ✅ Verified (White-as-builders still being confirmed).
- **William Skyrme (1778–1823) was born and died in the house** and was **Portreeve of Laugharne (1801, 1802, 1817).** The family's 65-ton sloop, the *William Skyrme*, appears in **Lloyd's Register** — the living link from the medieval quay to a working Georgian business. ✅ Verified.
- The **boundary wall still carries a dated plaque: "rebuilt by Maryabra Hughes Skyrme, AD 1837"** — naming the last heiress in the fabric itself. ✅ Verified.
- The line married into the **Wienholt** family, whose sons **emigrated to Queensland as major pastoralists** (Australian Dictionary of Biography). ✅ Verified.
- **Caleb Rees, Deputy Chief Inspector of Schools for Wales, lived here 1943–70**; his wife **Dr Laura Powell** was Medical Officer to the Welsh Board of Health. ✅ Verified residence · 🔶 the Powell/Caleb-Morris detail still to be logged to source.

## National figures in the house's orbit
- **Josiah Tucker** — Laugharne-born Dean of Gloucester and noted economist. **Sir John Perrot** — held the lordship of Laugharne. **Sir Sackville Crowe** — Treasurer of the Navy. **Sir John Powell** — one of the judges who acquitted the Seven Bishops (1688), buried at St Martin's. ✅ Verified as figures · 🔶 Powell's direct link to the *house* still to be firmed.

## The modern chapter — nailed to the record
- **Island House Restoration Ltd**, company no. **12452655**, is the confirmed owner (Companies House). ✅ Verified.
- **Percy Ll. Hughes-Garbett was resident at Island House c.1890–1893** — a name on nobody's timeline, confirmed from newspaper reports read at object level. ✅ Verified.
- The estate was **broken up by auction at Brown's Hotel in 1900**, having once covered an estimated **~15% of Laugharne Township.** 🔶 Referenced, pending the 1900 sale catalogue.

## And we cleaned up the myths (so the new story is trustworthy)
We also tested the old claims and corrected what was wrong: "Cromwell's cannon-balls" was **Rowland Laugharne, 1644**; the "Roman baths / Etruscan urn" is a **single Victorian sentence — tradition, not fact**; and the romantic **Skyrme–de Brian marriage**, the **Mansel lordship** and the **Crowe→Russell "sale"** don't stand. Kept as legend where charming, removed where false.

**In one line:** the house is a century older than the record claimed, with a proven medieval trading quay beneath it and a documented 800-year human story above it — a combination very few historic houses can evidence.
