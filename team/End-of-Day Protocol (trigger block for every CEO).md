## STANDING INSTRUCTION — END-OF-DAY WRITE-UP (save this and remember it)

**Trigger phrases:** "end of day write-up" · "EOD" · "wrap up the day" · "close the day".

When I say any of these, run the full process below **without me repeating it**. This is a standing instruction for this desk — keep it for the whole session.

### What you do when triggered

**1. LOG THE DAY TO AIRTABLE** (base `appnt9vSQKrKyaKiZ`) — write it, don't just tell me.

Create your row in the **Work Log** table (`tblDzFH9bNbhtZzkg`):
- **Date** = today · tag your **Business** · **Hours** = what you actually worked
- **Achievements** — concrete: what got produced, shipped or decided (bullets)
- **Report** — the fuller write-up: detail, movements forward, any big stage reached, decisions made, and options still open for me to choose
- **Summary** — one line on what the day was about

Add a line to **Daily Catch-Up / Handover** (`tblOJdMmjp5z7przv`), Type "Daily Catch-Up", dated today:
- **Done today** — the headline movements
- **Goals achieved / stage reached**
- **Decisions made** + **options pending** on me, Steve or Alys
- **Blockers** — what's stuck and on whom
- **Tomorrow** — the ranked task list you pick up first thing, written as "FIRST THING TOMORROW: …"

No Work Log row = your day shows as zero on the live hours page. This is not optional.

**2. SETTLE THE DAY — leave nothing loose:**
- **Ready to go live now?** Name the exact files/changes and hand them to the Master/Jarvis publisher. You do NOT push — the publisher does.
- **Queued for the morning?** Put it in your Handover line as "FIRST THING TOMORROW: …".
- Confirm nothing is half-finished that a fresh session tomorrow wouldn't understand.

**3. CONFIRM back to me** — your hours, your one-line summary, and what you've handed the publisher or queued for morning.

### Discipline
Never invent hours or work — log only what actually happened. If you didn't work today, log 0 and say so. Handover is the running narrative; the Work Log is the hours record that feeds the live site.
