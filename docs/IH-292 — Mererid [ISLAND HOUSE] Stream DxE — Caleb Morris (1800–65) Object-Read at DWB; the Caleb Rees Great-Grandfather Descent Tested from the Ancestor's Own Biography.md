## What the biography says (object-level)

Born **12 August 1800 at Parcyd, in the parish of Whitchurch, Pembrokeshire**. Educated at Cardigan, Haverfordwest, and Carmarthen grammar school. Received into church membership at **Pen-y-groes, Pembrokeshire**, aged 14, where he also began to preach. Carmarthen Academy, 1819. Called to **Narberth**, Pembrokeshire, 1822; ordained there **2 April 1823**. Moved to **Fetter Lane, London, in 1827**, where large crowds came to hear him. Invited to the principalship of the Independent College, Brecon, in 1838, but declined it. Periods of physical weakness sent him repeatedly back to Wales, and to Switzerland and Germany, for his health; he finally relinquished his church and retired to Pembrokeshire, where he **died 26 July 1865**. "He possessed unusual gifts as a preacher."

The article gives **no marriage, no wife, no children, and no descendants** of any kind.

## Finding

**A. The parish anchor is real (Verified — Fact recGzFwN0mP5bSMQW).**
Morris was born in the parish of **Whitchurch, Pembrokeshire** — the *same parish* as Caleb Rees's birthplace, Esgair-ordd, Whitchurch. The shared-Whitchurch-parish premise on which the descent tradition rests is now confirmed from the ancestor's own biography, not merely asserted. This is the positive half of the test.

**B. The genealogical step is uncorroborated from this end too (Verified — Fact reciY1oZKZ0NrEa5E).**
Because the DWB Morris article names no wife, children or descendants, it cannot corroborate or trace a line to Caleb Rees. The descent step therefore fails the object test from *both* directions: the Rees article (IH-291) named no great-grandfather, and the Morris article names no descendants. What survives is a consistent, honest negative on the *link*, with the *parish* anchor holding.

**C. Tier correction (recorded, not edited).**
The existing descent Fact **recX7Ysirft1smA8S** ("Caleb Morris … was Caleb Rees's great-grandfather") is in fact tiered **Referenced-pending** — its own notes read: "descent step circumstantial (shared Whitchurch parish + forename); Morris's biography names no descendants (audit Doc 118)." IH-291's handover mischaracterised it as "Verified-strong." **Referenced-pending is the correct tier and should stand.** The Fact is left untouched (additive-only); this note flags the record for the human review that IH-291 requested, and shows the review should *confirm* the existing tier rather than downgrade it.

A caution logged, not adopted: Morris's boyhood chapel was **Pen-y-groes, Pembrokeshire**, and Caleb Rees was a deacon at, and wrote a history of, a **Pen-y-groes** church (1959). These are almost certainly *different* Pen-y-groes places (the name is common; Rees's was in the Carmarthenshire/Port Talbot orbit), and a shared chapel name is precisely the kind of near-collision the method warns against. Recorded here only so a later run does not mistake it for evidence.
