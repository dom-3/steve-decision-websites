# Roster — Who's Who
*Read this before assigning a job to "someone". Almost everyone named in this operation is an **AI persona / desk**, not a real person. Confusing the two is how work gets mis-assigned.*

*Last updated 27 Jul 2026.*

---

## The rule
- **Personas** = defined AI seats with an operating pack in `team/`. You brief them; they draft; a human approves.
- **Real people** = Dominic, Steve, and named third parties. They decide, own, and send.
- **CEO decides · Jarvis coordinates · personas deliver · Dominic ships.**

## Common mix-ups (so they don't recur)
- **"Alex" is a persona, not one of Dominic's staff.** He's the B2B partnerships/copy seat.
- **The content engine is Nia's** (B2C), written to **Delphine's** voice — there is no "which person runs social" casting decision.
- **Only Jarvis pushes to the website repos** — no other desk runs git.
- **Nothing goes live or gets sent without Dominic's explicit approval tick.**
