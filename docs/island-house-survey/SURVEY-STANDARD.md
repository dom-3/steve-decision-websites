# Island House — Weekly Survey Standard (v1)

*The fixed standard for the weekly building survey. Its job: one capture, seen through every professional lens, to the same standard every week, so nothing is missed and an owner who is away can follow every detail. Authored 30 Jul 2026. Internal — not web-served (lives under `docs/`).*

---

## How a week runs (the workflow)

1. **Capture** — one continuous, slow 360° video walk of the site, following the architect's route. Export the stitched MP4; no editing.
2. **Frames** — sample frames (~every 0.5 m) → the week's numbered stations.
3. **Analyse** — run the nine voices below over the stations, each to its checklist.
4. **Publish** — build the dated week page (`island-house-survey-YYYY-MM-DD.html`): links to the 3D model + 360 walk-through, then the nine sections. Add the week to the hub.
5. **Owner** — clicks the date, reads the pack, sees progress vs last week.

## The shared standard — every section, every week

- **Dated + versioned** to the capture date.
- **RAG status** up top — 🔴 Red (act now / risk) · 🟠 Amber (watch / plan) · 🟢 Green (on track).
- **Cite the evidence** — every finding names its Station number(s) so it can be checked in the walk-through.
- **Since last week** — each section opens with what changed vs the previous week (Week 1 = baseline).
- **Actions** — each section ends with a short action list; anything needing the owner's decision or money is tagged **[OWNER DECISION]**.
- **Confidence + verify-on-site** — this is AI-assisted triage from imagery; it points the right professional at the right spot, it does not replace them. Structural, damp and conservation calls must be confirmed on site.
- **Record-before-cover** — anything historic about to be hidden by finishes is flagged for recording *this week*.

## Standing guardrails

- Not a substitute for a chartered surveyor, structural engineer, conservation specialist or Building Control.
- **No accessibility / step-free claims without an on-site audit.**
- Heritage: Grade II* listed — treat all historic fabric as protected; note listed-building-consent implications, never assert consent status.
- Flag, don't fix: we surface and prioritise; the professional signs off.

## The nine voices

Each voice has a **mission**, a **don't-miss checklist** (what it must scan for every week), and its **weekly output**.

### 1. The Owner's Executive Brief
- **Mission:** The one page a busy, absent owner reads first — what moved, what's urgent, what needs them.
- **Don't miss:** the week's headline; new 🔴 items; anything needing an [OWNER DECISION] or spend; overall RAG; "if you read nothing else."
- **Output:** ½–1 page. Top-line RAG, 3–5 bullets of what changed, the decisions awaiting the owner, and the single most important thing this week.

### 2. The Builder / Main Contractor
- **Mission:** What got done, what's happening now, what's next — buildability and workmanship.
- **Don't miss:** work completed since last week; active work areas; materials/plant on site; workmanship quality; sequencing/blockers; anything waiting on a decision or delivery.
- **Output:** progress narrative by area + a "this week / next week" list.

### 3. The Architect
- **Mission:** Is what's being built matching the drawings and the design intent?
- **Don't miss:** works vs the GA plans; deviations or ad-hoc changes; junctions/details that need a drawing; spatial quality; anything that should be an RFI.
- **Output:** conformance notes + queries to resolve on drawings.

### 4. The Structural Engineer
- **Mission:** Stability — is anything holding the building up temporary, moving, or cracking?
- **Don't miss:** temporary works (props, shoring, needles) and whether they're adequate/undisturbed; new steel/beams and their bearings; cracking (note pattern, location, whether active); lintels/openings; floor/roof loads; overloading from stored materials.
- **Output:** structural watch-list with RAG; explicit "needs engineer sign-off" items.

### 5. The Building Surveyor
- **Mission:** Overall condition and the running defects register.
- **Don't miss:** damp/staining; cracking; spalling; failed pointing/render; decay; water on floors; roof/rainwater goods; every defect logged with location + priority.
- **Output:** the condition schedule + the **prioritised Defects & Risk Register** (the master list all other voices feed).

### 6. The Damp & Timber Specialist
- **Mission:** Moisture and biological decay — the defining risk on this building.
- **Don't miss:** efflorescence (salts), green algae, tide-marks, standing water; penetrating vs rising vs condensation signals; ventilation; timber rot/wet-dry, insect attack; **breathability** — no non-breathable finishes trapping moisture in historic fabric; DPC/tanking questions.
- **Output:** moisture map by station + "find-and-fix-before-lining" actions.

### 7. The Conservation Officer + Historian (Mererid)
- **Mission:** Protect and record the historic fabric; tell the building's story as it's revealed.
- **Don't miss:** every historic feature (rubble stone, timber ceilings, reveals, fireplaces, lintels, plank doors, arches, niches, slit windows); **record-before-cover** items; old-vs-new junctions; conservation-appropriate repair (lime, breathable); anything with listed-building-consent implications; new historical evidence in the fabric (hand to Mererid).
- **Output:** the Heritage Features Record (photographed, located) + conservation cautions + a short "what the fabric told us this week" note.

### 8. Building Control / The Inspector
- **Mission:** Building Regulations compliance, stage-appropriate.
- **Don't miss:** structure (Part A); fire — compartmentation, escape, alarms (Part B); moisture/weather (Part C); ventilation (Part F); drainage (Part H); stairs/protection from falling (Part K); electrical (Part P); thermal only when at that stage (Parts L/M noted, not claimed). Flag likely inspection hold-points.
- **Output:** compliance watch-list + upcoming hold-points to book.

### 9. Health & Safety / CDM
- **Mission:** A safe site, week by week.
- **Don't miss:** trip hazards (cables, pipe stubs, stored materials); unguarded edges/openings/thresholds; working-at-height and scaffold; temporary electrics; dust/COSHH; fire points/extinguishers; access/egress; PPE/signage evidence.
- **Output:** hazard list with RAG + immediate-action items.

## Cadence of the "as-needed" voices (not every week)
- **Quantity Surveyor / Cost** — monthly, or when a finding hits budget.
- **Ecologist (bats/protected species)** — seasonal/legal triggers; flag an early bat check.
- **M&E / Services** — as first/second-fix progresses.
- **Fire strategy & Accessibility** — as the job moves toward fit-out (accessibility only with an on-site audit).
- **Energy/EPC, Archaeology** — later stage / only if triggered.
