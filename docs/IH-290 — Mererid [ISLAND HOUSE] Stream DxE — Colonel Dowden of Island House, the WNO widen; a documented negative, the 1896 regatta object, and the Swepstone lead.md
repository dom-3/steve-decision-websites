## 1. The new in-lane object — the 1896 regatta

**Source:** *The Carmarthen Weekly Reporter*, 14 August 1896 (Supplement), p.4, "Annual Regatta at Laugharne" — object-read in full (WNO `view/3582117/3582121/38`). Register source `recFKvnx2E8xBibll` (T1 primary / Newspaper / Read).

The regatta of Thursday 6 August 1896 lists its committee: *"The Portreeve; Capt. J N Harrison, J.P.; Mr P Ll Hughes-Garbett; Mr Lyon Falkener; … **Col. Dowden**; … Mr E W H Peel; …"* (President Mr H. F. Cooper; vice-president Mr W. Towers Smith).

This is the first time the register carries an August-1896 sighting of Dowden. It does three things:

- **Extends the documented window** to Aug 1895 → Jan 1896 → **Aug 1896** (Fact `recEFPXyioWM80FXH`, Verified).
- **Places the Island House tenant in the town's leading committee circle**, sitting beside the estate's own then-vendor Hughes-Garbett and the recurring Harrison/Peel names — a residence-and-standing signal, not a title to the house (Fact `recBxruvIJLK9GHrg`, Verified).
- **Still styles him only "Col. Dowden"** — no forename, no regiment.

## 2. The documented negative — the newspaper record cannot name him

Of the 18, only the three already-known Laugharne-town items concern the colonel (Infirmary Saturday, 9 Aug 1895; Christmas charity, 10 Jan 1896; regatta, 14 Aug 1896), and **each gives rank + surname only** ("Col. Dowden" / "Col, Mrs, and Miss Dowden"). The remainder are **page-level co-occurrence noise** — precisely Trap #2/#3 material:

**Finding (Fact `rec0zIBora1nqz2TM`, Verified):** the open newspaper record does **not** resolve Colonel Dowden's forename, initials or regiment. ## 3. The owner-narrative check

The owner's own compiled history — *"The Owners and People of Island House | 1778–2020"* (islandhouse.wales; source `rec7sGxZBNPVhNQ35`, T3 pointer / owner-narrative / Read) — runs Hughes-Garbett → **Captain Thomas Jones (1900)** → Schneider/Congreve (1903) → Morgan (1937) → Rose-Richards (1940) → Caleb Rees (1943) → Walters (1964), and **omits the Dowden tenancy entirely**.

**Finding (Fact `recwvgeeAVjJQxFBE`, Verified):** IH-289's newspaper-attested Dowden tenancy c.1895–96 is a genuine addition to the record — a tenant the owner's own researchers missed — not a restatement of an existing compiled history.

## 4. The lead, logged but NOT adopted

Among the Leicestershire noise sits a suggestive item: *Evening Express*, 22 May 1897, p.1 — *"Wanted immediately, Welsh Cook for … gentleman's family … Apply **Mrs Dowden, Swepstone, Ashby-de-la-Zouch, Leicestershire. Fare paid.**"* (source `reckJG7kDB38LfbiB`, T1 primary / Newspaper / Read).

A Mrs Dowden in Leicestershire seeking specifically a **Welsh** servant, **fare paid**, in 1897 is consistent with a family recently returned from Wales — which *would* date the Laugharne Dowdens' departure to c.1896–97 (after the Aug 1896 regatta, before the 1900 auction) and give a place to trace the colonel (Swepstone, near Ashby-de-la-Zouch).

But it is **not proof**. Held at **Referenced-pending** as a near-collision guard (Trap #3), **NOT adopted** as identification (Fact `recnTInoodZWdMq1Q`). A web search for a "Colonel Dowden of Swepstone" returned nothing; the lead must be tested on an archive day via Leicestershire directories / the 1901 census.
